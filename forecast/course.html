What this proof is testing

  1. Flip between Current and Next as often as you want before starting the swing.
  2. Green precipitation is the only playable surface. Your intended landing point is only a plan; the two meters determine where the ball actually arrives.
  3. The shot resolves against the visible next frame. Miss the moved radar and the ball falls through: land is +1, water is +2, and yellow-or-worse radar is a +1 storm hazard.
  4. After any penalty, the ball drops onto the nearest safe green radar on that new frame—never back onto an empty old lie.
  5. Flight and roll are separate. Club, power, accuracy, and each green surface determine the ball's remaining momentum.
  6. Rolling off the precipitation edge counts exactly like missing it in flight.
  7. Disconnected green radar islands are natural bonuses when they are large enough to hold the ball, reachable, closer to the cup than the ball, and not underneath the current lie. Smaller islands pay more.
  8. After every stroke, only a banked target's 55-degree umbrella remains on the current frame. Replay stroke temporarily restores the old targets and path; both disappear when replay ends. Previous stroke can inspect the path without restoring old targets.
  9. Replay stroke reruns the exact stored flight, roll, fall, and impact without changing the score or lie.
